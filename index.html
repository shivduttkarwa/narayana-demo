02 — Legacy

From seven students to six hundred thousand.

In 1979, Dr. P. Narayana opened a door in Nellore and seven students walked through it. Everything since has been an argument for what happens when you refuse to lower the bar.

1979

One room. Seven students.

Dr. P. Narayana, then a part‑time lecturer, opens a coaching centre in Nellore, Andhra Pradesh. The register has seven names on it.

1990s

Junior colleges arrive.

The model proves itself across Andhra Pradesh. Junior colleges follow the coaching centres, and the integrated method — board syllabus plus competitive prep — takes shape.

2000s

A national reputation.

Narayana becomes a household name for JEE and NEET preparation. Students from across India relocate to sit in its classrooms.

2010s

Schools, not just results.

The CBSE school network expands to 23 states. eKidz, eChamps and eTechno are authored — a curriculum that grows with the child rather than the exam calendar.

2020

The digital leap.

nLearn and nConnect go live. Lessons, tests, analytics and parent communication move onto one platform — and stay there long after the classrooms reopen.

2025

Fifty‑two new campuses.

One of the largest single‑year expansions in Indian schooling: 52 new campuses opened across 12 states, most of them in cities that had never had a school of this standard.

Today

950+ institutions. One promise.

Asia’s largest education network still runs on the sentence it started with: your dreams are our dreams.

03 — Academics

Four programmes. One continuous arc.

A child at five and a child at seventeen need entirely different things from a school. So we stopped pretending one curriculum could serve both.

A joyful start.

Pre‑primary · Ages 3–6

Child‑centric by design. Safe, hygienic, colour‑soaked spaces where the first years of school feel like play — because at this age, play is the syllabus.

  • Play‑led, activity‑first learning
  • Safe & hygienic dedicated spaces
  • Motor, language & social milestones
02 / eChamps

Curiosity, built to last.

Primary · Ages 6–11

The foundation years. Experiential and activity‑based, with hands‑on projects and tech‑enabled lessons that build conceptual understanding rather than recall.

  • Experiential, concept‑first method
  • Hands‑on projects & investigations
  • Tech‑enabled classroom lessons
03 / eTechno

Rigour, without the fear.

Middle & Secondary · Ages 11–16

CBSE depth kept fully intact, with link concepts woven in to lay real IIT‑JEE and Olympiad groundwork. Academic rigour, mental wellness and competitive exposure held in balance.

  • CBSE + link concepts, integrated

  • Wellness built into the timetable

04 — The Narayana Edge

Seven things we refuse to leave to chance.

01 / 07
  1. 01

    Integrated Curriculum

    CBSE, state board and competitive‑exam syllabi are not taught as three separate things. They are authored into one sequence, so a concept learned for the board exam is the same concept that answers a JEE question.

  2. 02

    Age‑Appropriate Programmes

    eKidz, eChamps, eTechno and Senior Secondary — four distinct programmes, each matched to a developmental stage rather than a convenient grade band.

  3. 03

    Well‑Planned Microschedules

    Every subject is broken into micro‑schedules that pace comprehension concept by concept. Nobody is asked to move on before the ground underneath them is solid.

  4. 04

    Pioneering R&D Team

    A full‑time academic research team authors personalised study plans from pre‑primary through higher secondary, and rewrites them as boards and entrance patterns shift.

  5. 05

    Adoption Calling

    One dedicated mentor per student, who calls the student and the parents every two weeks — not to report marks, but to find the problem before it becomes a pattern.

  6. 06

    Tech‑Enabled Framework

    nLearn, digital classrooms and performance analytics that make progress visible — to the teacher, to the student, and to the family at home.

  7. 07

    Our Fraternity

    50,000+ teachers and staff. The word we use internally is family, and the Adoption Calling programme is what stops that from being a poster on a wall.
